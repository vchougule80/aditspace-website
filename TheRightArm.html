The Right Arm

A Sci-Fi Adventure Story

Chapter 1

“Ok, we’re five more minutes away from here, so let’s quickly recap everything,” Theo said to his friends in the car, taking out his tablet.

The group was heading toward an abandoned hospital to rescue a boy named Henry. Theo explained the mission carefully while the others prepared supplies: antidotes, soccer balls, and medical equipment.

As they entered the gloomy hospital, strange clattering noises echoed through the halls. Broken shelves, shattered windows, and moss-covered walls surrounded them.

“This was Henry.”

A frightened, infected child emerged from the shadows with empty blue eyes.

Chapter 2

Mark attempted to calm Henry using a soccer ball and pretending to be his father. Henry slowly relaxed and smiled for the first time in a long while.

While Henry focused on the game, Max quietly approached from behind with the cure injector.

“Now!”

The cure was injected successfully, but Henry collapsed unconscious. Moments later, Mark suddenly fell to the ground suffering a seizure.

Chapter 3

Far away, political leaders debated the mysterious wormhole connected to the deadly Sermivirus.

Senator Ron argued that the wormhole should be sealed and controlled by the military, while Senator John believed it could help humanity.

“Think about all the possibilities!”

The debate grew intense as fears about the virus spread across Kuiyfog.

Chapter 4

After the meeting, John was rescued from an aggressive crowd by his wizard friend Alex, who carried him into the sky using magic.

The two discussed growing discrimination against magic users and the dangers of Senator Ron’s policies.

Chapter 5

Mark awoke from terrifying visions involving infected children chanting:

“He will destroy all!”

As the group traveled through the countryside, Mark hid a terrible secret from his friends.

He himself had been partly infected by the Sermivirus.

Chapter 6

The Right Arm encountered dangerous Patrollers while returning to base.

Later, at their hidden cabin headquarters, James revealed shocking news:

“You are being moved.”

The group would have to leave Kuiyfog entirely and travel overseas to Neward using fake passports.

Chapter 7

Senator Ron returned home exhausted and read the latest newspaper headlines.

The wormhole had become the biggest story across Kuiyfog, creating fear and uncertainty about the future.
